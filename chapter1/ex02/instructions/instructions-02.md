# The Situation:

You have a new position at a software-as-a-service (SaaS) company. Way to go!

Now that you're on the job, you need to familiarize yourself with the company's database. They generate all of their revenue through subscriptions so you'd like to start by checking out the subscriptions table. The thing is, you just want to see the structure of the table to get a feel for it. And the last thing you want to do is crash any servers on your first day on the job!
